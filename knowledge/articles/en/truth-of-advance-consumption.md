## 3. Four Classic Tricks of False Sales

### 3.1 Livestream Sales: The Three-Axe Routine

| Trick | Script | Essence |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **"Family!" (家人们)** | Manufactures closeness | Lowers your guard |
| **"Last X units!"** | Manufactures scarcity | Pushes impulsive orders |
| **"Original ¥9999, today ¥199"** | Fabricated original price | Pretending to discount, actually profiteering |

**Psychological basis:** Social proof + Scarcity effect. When the streamer says "last 3 units," your brain secretes cortisol, creating panic about "missing out." This isn't rational — it's physiological.

### 3.2 The Installment Trap

"Just the price of a bubble tea per day" —
Effective annual rate **18%-24%**. You pay more in interest than the principal.

**Real calculation:**

| Price | Periods | Per Period | Total Paid | Extra Cost | APR |

| ¥3,000 | 12 | ¥280 | ¥3,360 | ¥360 | 21.6% |
| ¥5,000 | 24 | ¥245 | ¥5,880 | ¥880 | 18.8% |
| ¥10,000 | 36 | ¥385 | ¥13,860 | ¥3,860 | 24.2% |

**What you thought was "easy installment" is actually an invisible chain shackled to your income.**

### 3.3 Advance Consumption Scripts

- "Be good to yourself while you're young" → Makes you think spending = self-love
- "Those who can spend can earn" → Dresses consumption as investment, confusing the direction
- "Enjoy now, pay later" → Makes you ignore future repayment pressure
- "Last day! Miss it, wait a year!" → Every day is the last day

These aren't suggestions. They're **scripts.**

### 3.4 Exploiting Peer Comparison

Result: bought something unusable. Forced to maintain face. Ended up as social burnout.

**Three stages of comparison-driven consumption:**
