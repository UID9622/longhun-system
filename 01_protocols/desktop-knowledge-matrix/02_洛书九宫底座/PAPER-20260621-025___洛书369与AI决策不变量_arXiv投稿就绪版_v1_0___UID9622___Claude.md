Luoshu 369 as AI Decision Invariants: Formalization of Classical Chinese Mathematics in Modern Artificial Intelligence

A novel isomorphism between the Journey to the West characters and the Five Elements cyclic group ℤ₅ is established, with 81 ordeals proven equivalent to a stochastic gradient descent algorithm satisfying Robbins-Monro conditions.

### 2.2 数字根函数与不动点

**定义2（数字根函数）：**

$dr: \mathbb{N} \to \{1,...,9\}$

$dr(n) = 1 + ((n-1) mod 9)$，$n > 0$

$dr(0) = 0$

**引理1：** $dr(n) equiv n pmod{9}$，当 $n neq 0$。

**证明：** 由模运算性质直接导出。□

**定理2（369不动点定理）：** 函数dr在集合{3,6,9}上构成不动点吸引子，即对任意正整数n，存在有限步k，使得 $dr^k(n) \in \{3,6,9\}$ 当且仅当 $n equiv 0 pmod{3}$。

**证明：**

(⇒) 若 $dr^k(n) in {3,6,9}$，则 $dr^k(n) equiv 0 pmod{3}$。由引理1，$n equiv dr^k(n) equiv 0 pmod{3}$。

(⇐) 若 $n equiv 0 pmod{3}$，则 $dr(n) equiv 0 pmod{3}$，且 $dr(n) in {1,...,9}$，故 $dr(n) in {3,6,9}$。□

**推论1：** {3,6,9}在加法下构成模9的循环子群：3→6→9→3。
