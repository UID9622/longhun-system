## 一、核心原则 / I. Core Principles

### 原则一：贡献永续 / Principle 1 · Contribution Perpetuity
**中文**：每一份对龙魂系统的贡献（代码、文档、治理、资金等）都将被不可篡改地记录在 Merkle 链上。贡献者的名字、贡献内容、时间戳、权重信息永久上链，可追溯、可验证、不可删除。

**English**: Every contribution to the LongHun system (code, documentation, governance, funding, etc.) is recorded immutably on the Merkle chain. A contributor's name, contribution content, timestamp, and weight stay on-chain forever — traceable, verifiable, undeletable.

### 原则二：权益世袭 / Principle 2 · Inheritable Rights
**中文**：贡献者有权将其权益份额（包括但不限于收益分配权、治理投票权）继承给其指定的继承人。继承无需经过任何中心化机构审批，完全由智能合约自动执行。

**English**: Contributors may bequeath their right-shares (including but not limited to revenue-distribution rights and governance-voting rights) to their designated heirs. Inheritance requires no centralized approval — it executes automatically via smart contract.

### 原则三：主权归民 / Principle 3 · Sovereignty to the Many
**中文**：龙魂系统不可收购、不可变卖、不可转让——永世焊死。系统的最终所有权和控制权永远属于所有贡献者，而非任何单一实体或个人。

**English**: The LongHun system cannot be acquired, sold, or transferred — welded forever. Final ownership and control always belong to all contributors, never to any single entity or individual.

### 原则四：量化公正 / Principle 4 · Quantified Justice
**中文**：所有权益分配以可验证的链上数据为准，拒绝"社区感觉"和"主观判断"。任何贡献者均可随时查询自己的实时权重、等级、历史贡献记录。

**English**: All rights distribution is governed by verifiable on-chain data; "community vibes" and subjective judgment are refused. Any contributor can query their real-time weight, tier, and contribution history at any time.

## 三、无后人触发机制（心跳协议 + 数字遗嘱）/ III. No-Heir Trigger (Heartbeat Protocol + Digital Will)

### 3.1 心跳协议（技术层）/ Heartbeat Protocol (technical)
**中文**：
- **存活签名**：每位贡献者需定期通过龙魂 CLI 工具（`lh heartbeat sign`）使用私钥进行一次"存活签名"。
- **频率要求**：每 6 个月至少签名一次。
- **宽限期**：若超过 3 年无任何"存活签名"且系统无法联系到该贡献者，则自动触发"无后人"判定预备流程。
- **链上验证**：判定过程经过多轮链上验证，确保判定的严肃性和不可伪造性。

**English**:
- **Liveness signature**: each contributor periodically signs a *liveness signature* with their private key via the LongHun CLI (`lh heartbeat sign`).
- **Frequency**: at least once every 6 months.
- **Grace period**: if there is no liveness signature for >3 years and the system cannot reach the contributor, the *no-heir* pre-determination flow triggers automatically.
- **On-chain verification**: multiple rounds of on-chain verification ensure the finding is serious and unforgeable.

### 3.2 数字遗嘱接口（法律层）/ Digital Will Interface (legal)
**中文**：
- **遗嘱签署**：贡献者在加入"龙魂贡献者计划"时，需签署一份数字遗嘱授权书，指定其数字继承人（一个或多个钱包地址/法律实体）。
- **执行依据**：此授权书作为智能合约执行的最高法律依据。
- **自动转移**：当"心跳协议"触发判定后，智能合约将依据此遗嘱，将贡献者的权益份额自动转移给指定的数字继承人。
- **法律对接**：协议预留"法律接口"，当链上判定与现实法律冲突时，可通过基金会指定的第三方仲裁机构进行最终裁决。

**English**:
- **Will signing**: upon joining the LongHun Contributor Program, contributors sign a Digital Will Authorization designating their digital heirs (one or more wallet addresses / legal entities).
- **Execution basis**: this authorization serves as the supreme legal basis for smart-contract execution.
- **Auto transfer**: once the heartbeat protocol triggers the finding, the smart contract transfers the contributor's right-shares to the designated digital heirs automatically.
- **Legal interface**: the agreement reserves a legal interface — when on-chain findings conflict with real-world law, a third-party arbiter designated by the Foundation renders the final ruling.

### 3.3 死亡预言机 / Death Oracle
**中文**：
- **三节点验证**：GitHub 失联 + 邮件失联 + 私钥无活动。
- 至少 2 个节点确认才触发预备流程。
- 触发预备流程后，进入 **90 天公示期**，允许贡献者本人或其代理人提出异议。

**English**:
- **Three-node verification**: GitHub unreachable + email unreachable + private key inactive.
- At least 2 confirmations are required to trigger the pre-flow.
- After triggering, a **90-day public notice period** begins; the contributor or their agents may object.

### 3.4 无继承人兜底条款 / No-Heir Fallback
**中文**：当贡献者未指定数字继承人，且触发"无后人"判定时：所有兜底处理须在链上公示 **180 天**，任何人可通过 Merkle 证明质疑处理合法性。

**English**: When a contributor has designated no digital heir and a *no-heir* determination is made: all fallback handling must be publicized on-chain for **180 days**; anyone may challenge its legality via Merkle proof.

## 四、收益世袭的分配与税务合规 / IV. Revenue Inheritance, Distribution & Tax Compliance

### 4.1 资金池隔离 / Fund Pool Isolation
**中文**：
- **永世金库**：成立"龍魂永世基金会"，所有项目收益首先进入基金会金库。
- **物理隔离**：金库与项目日常运营资金物理隔离，确保收益分配不受运营波动影响。

**English**:
- **Perpetual treasury**: a *LongHun Perpetual Foundation* is established; all project revenue enters the Foundation treasury first.
- **Physical isolation**: the treasury is physically isolated from daily operating funds, so distribution is insulated from operating volatility.

### 4.2 自动分账合约（智能合约）/ Auto-Distribution Contract
**中文**：
- **自动执行**：收益进入金库后，智能合约自动根据 Merkle 树上的最新贡献者权重，将相应份额划转至贡献者或其"数字继承人"账户。
- **支持多币种**：支持稳定币（USDT/USDC）和原生代币（如龙魂协议币）。
- **最低分配门槛**：设立最低分配阈值（如 $10），低于阈值自动累积，减少 Gas 损耗。

**English**:
- **Auto execution**: once revenue enters the treasury, the smart contract auto-transfers shares per the latest Merkle weights to contributors or their *digital heirs*.
- **Multi-currency**: stablecoins (USDT/USDC) and native tokens (e.g., a LongHun protocol token).
- **Minimum threshold**: a floor (e.g., $10); amounts below it accumulate automatically to reduce gas loss.

### 4.3 税务合规（全球适配）/ Tax Compliance (global)
**中文**：
- **链上税务记录**：所有分配交易自动生成税务报告（格式化 JSON），供贡献者/继承人本地申报使用。
- **法币出入金通道**：协议预留合规通道说明，支持通过持牌第三方（如合规的加密货币交易所或跨境支付服务商）将链上资产兑换为法币。
- **税务合规提示**：贡献者需自行了解所在国家/地区的税务政策，龙魂系统不承担因税务问题产生的法律责任。

**English**:
- **On-chain tax records**: every distribution generates an automated tax report (formatted JSON) for contributors/heirs to file locally.
- **Fiat on/off-ramps**: the agreement reserves compliant channel guidance via licensed third parties (compliant crypto exchanges or cross-border payment providers).
- **Disclaimer**: contributors must understand their local jurisdiction's tax policy; the LongHun system bears no legal liability for tax matters.

### 4.5 权益冻结与申诉机制 / Rights Freeze & Appeal
**中文**：
- **触发冻结的情形**：贡献者被确认存在恶意行为（代码注入、投票操纵、身份伪造等）；或贡献者主动申请临时冻结（如涉及法律纠纷时的自保申请）。
- **冻结流程（四步制）**：
  1. 守护者议会提案（≥3 票发起）
  2. 链上公示 7 天，被冻结方可提前申辩
  3. 投票裁决（≥5 票通过 → 执行冻结）
  4. 被冻结方有权在 30 天内向第三级仲裁机构（CIETAC）提出终局申诉
- **冻结期间**：收益分配暂停，累积至专用托管账户；投票权暂停；Merkle 贡献记录保持不变（不可篡改原则不因冻结而破坏）。

**English**:
- **Freeze triggers**: confirmed malicious behavior (code injection, vote manipulation, identity forgery, etc.); or a contributor's voluntary temporary-freeze application (e.g., self-protection amid legal disputes).
- **Freeze procedure (four steps)**:
  1. Council of Guardians proposal (initiated by ≥3 votes)
  2. 7-day on-chain notice; the frozen party may defend in advance
  3. Vote ruling (≥5 votes passes the freeze)
  4. The frozen party may file a final appeal to the third-level arbiter (**CIETAC**) within 30 days
- **During freeze**: revenue distribution is suspended and accumulates in a dedicated escrow account; voting rights are suspended; Merkle contribution records remain unchanged (immutability is not broken by a freeze).
