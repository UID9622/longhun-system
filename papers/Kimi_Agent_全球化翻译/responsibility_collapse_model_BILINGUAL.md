### Abstract | 摘要

本文提出"责任塌缩概率模型"（Responsibility Collapse Model），将日常观察中的"事不关己"现象转化为可计算的概率行为体框架。核心命题为：人不是本质上的善恶体，而是环境驱动下的概率行为体；"事不关己"不是道德缺陷，而是风险-收益不对称的理性响应。本文通过七因子责任轴（R1–R7）构建责任系数 R（Responsibility Coefficient R），建立三色阈值体系（Three-Tier Thresholds）将责任水平量化为绿/黄/红三档；提出家人软肋系数 γ_family（Family Vulnerability Coefficient）解释极端胁迫下的责任偏离；论证互联网通过传播系数、匿名系数与算法放大的三重叠加产生约1000倍的责任放大效应；并以易经动态平衡思想与95%-5%文明安全定律（95-5% Civilization Safety Law）为文明设计提供目标函数。本文的目标是证明：让善良有生存空间，是一个可工程化的体系设计问题，而非单纯的道德呼吁。

This paper presents the Responsibility Collapse Model, transforming the everyday observation of "not my problem" into a computable probabilistic agent framework. The core propositions are: humans are not intrinsically good or evil, but environmentally-driven probabilistic agents; "not my problem" is not a moral defect but a rational response to risk-reward asymmetry. Through the seven-factor responsibility axis (R1–R7), we construct the Responsibility Coefficient R and establish the Three-Tier Thresholds to quantify responsibility levels into green/yellow/red zones. We introduce the Family Vulnerability Coefficient γ_family to explain responsibility deviation under extreme coercion, and demonstrate that the Internet produces approximately 1000× responsibility amplification through the triple multiplication of propagation, anonymity, and algorithmic amplification. Drawing on I Ching dynamic balance philosophy and the 95-5% Civilization Safety Law, we provide an objective function for civilization design. The goal of this paper is to prove: making room for goodness is an engineering design problem, not merely a moral appeal.

### §2 Probabilistic Agent Model | 概率行为体模型

#### §2.1 Basic Formula | 基本公式

**人的行为可以被建模为环境条件下的条件概率。**
*Human behavior can be modeled as a conditional probability under environmental constraints.*

```
P(kindness | environment) = P₀(personal baseline) × [reward(kindness) / risk(kindness)]ˣ

P(善行 | 环境) = P₀(个人基率) × [reward(善行) / risk(善行)]ˣ

where x ∈ [0.5, 3.0] is the environmental pressure coefficient.
其中 x ∈ [0.5, 3.0] 为环境压力系数。
```

**Corollaries | 推论：**

1. **Collapse Condition | 塌缩条件**：When risk(kindness) > reward(kindness) and x is sufficiently large, P(kindness) → 0. *当 risk(善行) > reward(善行) 且 x 足够大时，P(善行) → 0。* This is the mathematical expression of responsibility collapse: the probability of responsible behavior approaches zero under unfavorable conditions.

2. **Flourishing Condition | 繁荣条件**：When reward(kindness) > risk(kindness), P(kindness) → 1. *当 reward(善行) > risk(善行) 时，P(善行) → 1。* When goodness is rewarded, responsibility becomes the default behavior.

3. **Critical Balance Point | 临界平衡点**：The point where P₀ × reward / risk = 1 represents the critical balance point of civilization. *P₀ × reward / risk = 1 → 这是文明的临界平衡点。* At this exact equilibrium, the incentive structure neither promotes nor suppresses responsible behavior.

### §4 Responsibility Coefficient R and Three-Tier Thresholds | 责任系数R与三色阈值

*责任系数 R 是一个综合指标，以经验导出的权重聚合七个因子：*

```
R = (R2_acuity_at_critical × 0.4)
  + (R6_long_term_value_weight × 0.4)
  + (R3_semantic_density_at_critical × 0.2)
  − (R1_absence_rate_at_critical × 0.5)
  − (R5_pleasing_word_frequency × 0.3)

**Weight Rationale | 权重说明：**

risk(善行)_线上 = constant × α(传播系数) × β(匿名系数) × γ(算法放大)

When α → 10, β → 10, γ → 10:
  risk_online ≈ 1000 × risk_offline

当三个系数都趋近10时：

```
