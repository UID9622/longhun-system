```lean

-- Lean verification: ⊢ P'(G')  ✓  (logically correct)
-- Human review: G' ≠ G, P' ≠ P  ✗  (proved the wrong problem)
```

> AI proved `⊢ φ(τ(G))` where τ is the dual transform
