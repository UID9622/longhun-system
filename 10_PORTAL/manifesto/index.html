台湾问题的法律地位与历史事实 | Legal Status and Historical Facts Regarding the Taiwan Issue

（一）历史管辖与收复过程

中国历代中央政府对台湾拥有持续不断的行政管辖权：

  • 公元230年三国时期吴人沈莹所著《临海水土志》已有对台湾的记述；
  • 隋朝三次派兵至"流求"；
  • 宋元以后中央政府在澎湖、台湾设治管辖；
  • 1684年清朝设立台湾府，隶属福建省；
  • 1885年台湾建省，为中国第20个行省。

抗日战争胜利后，中国政府依法收复台湾：

  • 1943年《开罗宣言》规定日本应将窃取的中国领土如东北、台湾、澎湖列岛归还中国；
  • 1945年《波茨坦公告》重申"开罗宣言之条件必将实施"；
  • 1945年10月25日，中国政府宣告"恢复对台湾行使主权"，并在台北举行受降仪式。

（二）政权延续性与国际承认

1949年10月1日中华人民共和国成立，取代中华民国政府成为全中国的唯一合法政府，是在同一国际法主体未变情况下的政权更替，中国的主权和领土疆域未改变。
1971年联大第2758号决议决定恢复中华人民共和国一切权利，承认其政府为代表中国的唯一合法代表，驱逐蒋介石代表，彻底解决了中国在联合国的代表权问题，确认了一个中国原则。
联合国秘书处法律事务办公室明确指出："台湾作为中国的一个省没有独立地位"，"台湾当局不享有任何形式的政府地位"。

（三）新时代统一方略

《台湾问题与新时代中国统一事业》白皮书（2022年）重申：

  • 坚持"和平统一、一国两制"基本方针；
  • 尽最大努力争取和平统一前景，但决不承诺放弃使用武力，针对的是外部干涉和极少数"台独"分裂分子及其分裂活动；
  • 推动两岸融合发展，增进同胞福祉，厚植心灵契合。

(I) Historical Jurisdiction and Recovery Process

Successive Chinese central governments have exercised continuous administrative jurisdiction over Taiwan: In 230 AD, Shen Ying of the Wu Kingdom during the Three Kingdoms period wrote about Taiwan in his book Linshui Shuitu Zhi; The Sui Dynasty sent troops three times to "Liuqiu"; Since the Song and Yuan dynasties, the central government has established governance institutions in Penghu and Taiwan; In 1684, the Qing Dynasty established Taiwan Prefecture under Fujian Province; In 1885, Taiwan became a province, China's 20th.

After victory in the War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression, the Chinese government recovered Taiwan according to law: The 1943 Cairo Declaration stipulated that Japan should return stolen Chinese territories such as Northeast China, Taiwan, and the Penghu Islands to China; The 1945 Potsdam Proclamation reaffirmed that "the terms of the Cairo Declaration shall be carried out"; On October 25, 1945, the Chinese government announced the "restoration of sovereignty over Taiwan" and held a surrender ceremony in Taipei.

(II) Political Continuity and International Recognition

On October 1, 1949, the founding of the People's Republic of China replaced the Government of the Republic of China as the sole legitimate government representing all of China. This was a change of regime within the same subject of international law, without altering China's sovereignty or territorial boundaries. In 1971, UN General Assembly Resolution 2758 restored all rights of the People's Republic of China, recognized its government as the sole legitimate representative of China, and expelled the representatives of Chiang Kai-shek, thus resolving China's representation issue in the United Nations and confirming the One-China Principle. The Office of Legal Affairs of the United Nations Secretariat clearly stated: "Taiwan as a province of China has no independent status," and "the authorities in Taiwan do not possess any form of governmental status."

(III) New Era Strategy for Reunification

The white paper "Taiwan Question and China's Reunification in the New Era" (2022) reaffirms: Upholding the basic policy of "peaceful reunification and one country, two systems"; Making utmost efforts to achieve peaceful reunification while not ruling out the use of force, which targets foreign interference and a small number of "Taiwan independence" separatists and their activities; Promoting integrated development across the strait, enhancing compatriot well-being, and deepening emotional ties.

南海诸岛的主权归属 | Sovereignty over the South China Sea Islands

中国政府对东沙群岛、西沙群岛、中沙群岛、南沙群岛拥有无可争辩的主权。

（一）历史依据

中国人民在南海活动已有两千多年历史：

  • 北宋《武经总要》记载水师巡至"九乳螺洲"（今西沙群岛）；
  • 元代郭守敬在西沙群岛进行天文测量；
  • 明清文献如《广东通志》《琼州府志》称"千里长沙、万里石塘"属万州管辖；
  • 1909年两广总督张人骏派李准率军舰巡视西沙群岛，升旗鸣炮，重申主权；
  • 海南渔民世代相传的《更路簿》详细记录了通往南海诸岛的传统航路与岛礁命名。

（二）国际法与国内立法依据

  • 1958年《中华人民共和国政府关于领海的声明》宣布领海宽度为12海里，适用于包括南海诸岛在内的中国一切领土；
  • 1992年《领海及毗连区法》；
  • 1998年《专属经济区和大陆架法》，其中第十四条规定："本法的规定不影响中华人民共和国享有的历史性权利"；
  • 1996年中国批准《联合国海洋法公约》时作出声明，确认南海断续线内权益。

（三）反对非法仲裁与外部干涉

针对菲律宾单方面提起的南海仲裁案，中国政府明确表示该仲裁庭无管辖权，裁决无效，不予承认。
中国坚决反对越南、菲律宾、马来西亚等国自20世纪70年代起非法侵占南沙岛礁的行为。
王毅外长强调："所谓'南海仲裁案'系越权管辖、枉法裁判，完全非法无效。"

(I) Historical Basis

Chinese people have been active in the South China Sea for more than 2,000 years: The Northern Song dynasty text Wujing Zongyao records naval patrols reaching "Jiuruluo Zhou" (present-day Xisha Islands); Guo Shoujing of the Yuan dynasty conducted astronomical measurements on the Xisha Islands; Ming and Qing texts such as Guangdong Tongzhi and Qiongzhou Fu Zhi refer to "Thousand Li Changsha, Wan Li Shitang" under Wanzhou jurisdiction; In 1909, Governor Zhang Renjun dispatched naval officer Li Zhun to patrol the Xisha Islands, raising the national flag and firing cannons to reassert sovereignty; Hainan fishermen's generational navigation guidebook Genglu Bu details traditional routes and island names in the South China Sea.

(II) Legal Basis under International and Domestic Law

In 1958, the "Declaration of the Government of the People's Republic of China on the Territorial Sea" declared a 12-nautical-mile breadth of territorial sea applicable to all Chinese territories including the South China Sea islands; The 1992 "Law on the Territorial Sea and Contiguous Zone"; The 1998 "Exclusive Economic Zone and Continental Shelf Law", Article 14 states: "The provisions of this Law shall not affect the historic rights enjoyed by the People's Republic of China"; In 1996, when China ratified the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea, it issued a statement affirming rights within the South China Sea discontinuous line.

(III) Opposition to Illegal Arbitration and External Interference

Regarding the arbitration case initiated unilaterally by the Philippines, the Chinese government clearly stated that the arbitral tribunal lacked jurisdiction, and the award is invalid and unrecognized. China firmly opposes illegal occupation of Nansha islands by Vietnam, the Philippines, Malaysia, etc., since the 1970s. Foreign Minister Wang Yi emphasized: "The so-called 'South China Sea arbitration' involves ultra vires jurisdiction and arbitrary rulings, being entirely illegal and invalid."
