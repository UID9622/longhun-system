FAQ
## When and where is OpenAI DevDay? ( ↓ )
OpenAI DevDay is on October 6, 2025 at Fort Mason in San Francisco.
## How do I register?( ↓ )
If you submitted to attend onsite during our open submission period, we will be updating you on your status by mid-August.
Did you receive an invitation? Click the link in your email to access the in-person experience, or reach out to us at devday@openai.com
## Will there be tickets available at the door?( ↓ )
Sadly, no. All attendees need a confirmed ticket and invitation from OpenAI ahead of time.
## Will there be a livestream?( ↓ )
The opening session will be livestreamed. All other sessions will be recorded and posted to OpenAI.com shortly after OpenAI DevDay. Sign up and we will send you details if we’re not able to host you in person for the event.
## I’m a member of press/media. Can I attend?( ↓ )
For press who are interested in attending in person, please reach out to  press@openai.com.
## I got a ticket. Can I give my ticket to a friend or colleague?( ↓ )
OpenAI DevDay tickets are non-transferable and not for resale. You can learn more in our Event Terms & Conditions. If you have any special circumstances, please reach out to us at devday@openai.com, we’re here to help.
If you have other questions that still need to be answered, contact us at devday@openai.com.
