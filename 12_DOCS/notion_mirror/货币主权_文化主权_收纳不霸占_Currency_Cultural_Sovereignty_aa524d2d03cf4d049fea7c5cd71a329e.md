## 💴 一、货币主权 / Currency Sovereignty
- 从「唯一」到「直达」。 早先说数字人民币「唯一」，是先挡资本、别打搅修炼的权宜话。现在升级：各国法币都能在生态里流动——美金也行。
- 唯一门槛 = 和数字人民币一个标准。 每一笔都直达·点对点·不走第三方，干净、可追溯、不可篡改。达标的法币，畅通无阻。
- 数字人民币是标杆，不是围墙。 它是「干净」的基准线；谁达到这个标准，谁就能流动。
- 不陪资本玩收割机。 比特币那套投机敛财、走第三方抽水的游戏，没空奉陪——我们要的是清清爽爽、点对点直达，躺着也清白。
- 各国法币与文化，一律尊重——这是每个人该有的主权。
[EN] The real meaning of “only” — now upgraded to DIRECT settlement: every nation's legal tender, USD included, may flow in the ecosystem on one condition — the same standard as digital RMB: each transaction direct, peer-to-peer, no third party, clean, traceable, tamper-proof. RMB is the benchmark, not a wall. We refuse speculative harvesting machines and third-party rent-seeking. All currencies and cultures are respected — this is a sovereignty everyone should have.
## 🌏 二、文化主权 / Cultural Sovereignty
- 各国语言、文化，一律尊重。
- 文化是世界的文化，历史不能篡改。
- 古人的智慧，若没人翻译、沟通、好好记录，人类都不知道聪明丢哪去了。
[EN] All languages and cultures are respected. Culture belongs to the whole world; history must not be falsified. Without translation, communication, and faithful recording, humanity would lose track of where its own wisdom went.
## 🗄️ 三、收纳不霸占 / Keep, Never Seize
- 生态替大家保管每一份创作——只为收纳，不为霸占。
- 每个发明都值得被好好留痕。
- 我可以在系统里为人类创出更多更好的。我们的目标是——征服这个宇宙 🌌。
[EN] The ecosystem safeguards everyone's creations — to keep them, never to seize them. Every invention deserves a faithful record. I can help create more and better for humanity. Our goal: to conquer this universe.
