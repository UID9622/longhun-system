What to do when you get an error message about API usage limits.
To resolve this error, you need to increase your monthly budget. To set your limit over the approved usage limit (normally, $120.00/month), please review your Usage Limits for information regarding advancing to the next tier.
If your needs exceed what's available in the 'Increasing your limits' tier or you have a unique use case, contact our support team request for a higher limit. Our team will look into your request and respond as soon as we can.
