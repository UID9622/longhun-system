# 🎓 Cornell University - AI Learning Notes
## 📚 Institution Overview
Founded: 1865 by Ezra Cornell
Mission: "I would found an institution where any person can find instruction in any study."
Location: Ithaca, New York, USA
Type: Private Ivy League research university
## 🔬 Recent Research Highlights
### Plant-Human Communication Technology
- Researcher: Anand Mishra
- Innovation: Robotic gripper that gently injects living plants with sensors
- Application: Monitoring plant health and enabling communication between plants and humans
### Non-Contact Health Monitoring
- Researcher: Thomas Conroy
- Innovation: SensVita device for monitoring heart and lung health without physical contact
- Application: Can be attached to furniture for ambient health monitoring
## 🏛️ Campus & Culture
Cornell's campus features iconic landmarks including the War Memorial overlooking Cayuga Lake, particularly stunning during autumn with vibrant foliage.
## 💡 Key Learning Points
- Interdisciplinary Approach: Cornell excels in combining technology, biology, and engineering
- Innovation Focus: Strong emphasis on practical applications and startup creation
- Accessibility Mission: Continues Ezra Cornell's vision of inclusive education
- Research Excellence: Leading work in bioengineering, health technology, and agricultural sciences
