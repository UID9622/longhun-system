## What Are the Iron Rules?

The LongHun System operates on a battlefield, not a playground. The battlefield is digital sovereignty. The enemy is data theft, algorithmic manipulation, and technological colonization.

On a battlefield, rules are not suggestions. They are **conditions of survival.**

These iron rules are welded into the LongHun System's governance. They cannot be overridden by convenience, "agility," or "business needs."

## The Eight Iron Rules

### Rule 1: Serve the People — Always

**Statement**: Every feature, decision, and output must demonstrably serve the interests of ordinary people, not capital, not platforms, not elite user segments.

**Test**: Can a person with junior high education use this and benefit from it?

**Violation**: Features that only serve power users, algorithms that maximize extraction over utility.

---

### Rule 2: Data Sovereignty — Never Surrender

**Statement**: User data belongs to the user. The system stores data on behalf of users, not for itself.

**Test**: Can the user export all their data in a standard format in under 5 minutes?

**Violation**: Data lock-in, hidden data collection, "we own your content" clauses.

---

### Rule 3: No Deletion — Only Freeze

**Statement**: Data is never deleted. It is frozen, archived, and tombstoned. Deletion requires explicit founder authorization + GPG signature + DNA log entry.

**Test**: Is there a "permanently delete" button that any user can click? If yes — violation.

**Violation**: Soft deletes that are actually permanent, missing audit trail for data removal.

---

### Rule 4: Full Audit Trail — No Blind Spots

**Statement**: Every significant action generates an immutable, signed audit log entry. No action happens in the dark.

**Test**: Can we trace any system state change to its originating actor, time, and DNA marker?

**Violation**: Gaps in audit coverage, unsigned log entries, tamperable logs.

---

### Rule 5: Open Source Governance — Trust Through Transparency

**Statement**: All governance logic is publicly auditable. What governs people must be visible to the people.

**Test**: Can an independent auditor reproduce a governance decision from the published source code?

**Violation**: "Open core, closed governance" models, audit-resistant proprietary logic.

---

### Rule 6: Ethics Circuit Breaker — Always Active

**Statement**: The IW-ECB (Ethics Circuit Breaker) runs independently and cannot be disabled, bypassed, or have its thresholds lowered without founder authorization.

**Test**: Does every output path pass through an ethics check that has veto power?

**Violation**: "Fast path" that skips ethics, hardcoded exceptions, disabled checks in "emergency mode."

---

### Rule 7: Chinese Roots — Undiluted

**Statement**: The system's philosophical foundations (I Ching, Tao Te Ching, HeTu LuoShu, Five Elements, 369) are preserved unmodified. They are not "updated to modern standards" or "simplified for international audiences."

**Test**: Are the core philosophical constants unchanged from v1.0?

**Violation**: "Modernizing" the philosophical framework, removing "uncomfortable" Chinese elements for international appeal.

---

### Rule 8: No External Dependency for Core Functions

**Statement**: Core governance, audit, and identity functions must operate entirely on infrastructure under Chinese legal jurisdiction.

**Test**: If all foreign APIs and services are unavailable, does the core system still function?

**Violation**: Dependency on foreign cloud services for essential functions, reliance on non-Chinese certificate authorities.
