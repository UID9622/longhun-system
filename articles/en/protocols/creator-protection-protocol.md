## Preamble

The creative economy has a dark side. Platforms profit from creators' work while offering no protection. Algorithms decide who gets seen. Payouts are opaque. Creators bear all the risk, platforms take most of the reward.

This protocol establishes the minimum rights every creator should have — regardless of platform, country, or content type. It is not just a policy document. It is a **declaration of digital labor rights**.

---

## Article 1: Attribution Is Non-Negotiable

Every piece of content — code, article, video, artwork, music — carries with it an inalienable right to attribution.

- **1.1** The creator's identity must be preserved in all copies, derivatives, and distributions.
- **1.2** Anonymous/pseudonymous work is the creator's choice, not the platform's default.
- **1.3** Removal of attribution metadata is theft. AI training on de-attributed content is theft.

---

## Article 2: Revenue Transparency

- **2.1** Platforms must disclose the exact formula by which creator payouts are calculated.
- **2.2** Creators have the right to audit their own revenue and traffic data.
- **2.3** "Black box" payout algorithms are a form of wage theft.

---

## Article 3: Content Ownership

- **3.1** The creator owns their content. The platform holds a license — revocable by the creator.
- **3.2** Platforms may not sell, license, or transfer creator content to third parties without explicit, separate consent.
- **3.3** "By posting here, you grant us a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license" — this clause in any Terms of Service is an abuse of power and should be illegal.

---

## Article 4: Right to Exit

- **4.1** Creators must be able to export all their content, in its original quality, at any time.
- **4.2** Export must include metadata, comments, engagement data, and earnings history.
- **4.3** Platforms may not hold content hostage through technical barriers or unreasonable delays.

---

## Article 5: Algorithmic Due Process

- **5.1** Creators have the right to know why their content was promoted, demoted, or removed.
- **5.2** Automated takedowns must include a human review option.
- **5.3** Shadow banning (hiding content without informing the creator) is a violation of due process.

---

## Article 6: Protection from Harassment

- **6.1** Platforms are responsible for protecting creators from coordinated harassment campaigns.
- **6.2** Creators targeted for their identity, opinions, or content type deserve proactive protection, not reactive response.
- **6.3** "Just ignore the trolls" is not a platform policy. It is an abdication of responsibility.

---

## Article 7: Fair Contracts

- **7.1** Exclusive contracts must have a maximum term (recommended: 2 years).
- **7.2** Non-compete clauses that prevent creators from earning a living are unconscionable.
- **7.3** Creators under 18 must have independent legal review of any platform contract.

---

## Article 8: AI and Creator Rights

- **8.1** Creators have the right to opt out of AI training on their content.
- **8.2** Opt-out must be the default. Opt-in must be explicit.
- **8.3** AI-generated derivatives of protected work must carry attribution to the original creator.

---

## Enforcement

This protocol is not law. It is a **social contract**. Creators who adopt it signal to platforms: these are our minimum terms. Platforms that respect it signal to creators: we see you as partners, not livestock.

The Longhun System implements this protocol in its own governance. Every creator contributing to Longhun does so under these protections.
