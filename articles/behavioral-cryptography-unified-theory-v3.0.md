## 2. Left Wing · Seven-Factor Provenance

### 2.1 Core Principle

Behavioral cryptography does not ask "is this AI-generated?" but rather "can the origin of this content be proven?"

Seven behavioral factors are the author's subconscious imprints—a single factor can be mimicked, but the probability of simultaneously forging all seven factors approaches zero.

### 2.2 Seven-Factor Definitions

| # | Factor | Weight | Anti-Forgery Logic | Forgery Cost |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|:---:|
| F1 | Identity DNA | 0.20 | Signature patterns · opening/closing habits · punctuation preferences | Requires full replication of creative habits |
| F2 | Temporal Anchor | 0.15 | Blockchain-style timestamp chain · Ganzhi quadruple pillars | Requires forging continuous time series |
| F3 | Content Hash | 0.18 | SM3 national cryptographic hash · Merkle tree root | One character change → hash completely different |
| F4 | Style Vector | 0.17 | Sentence length distribution · word frequency patterns · paragraph structure | Requires deep learning + long-term training |
| F5 | Protected Vocabulary | 0.12 | Author-unique high-frequency term preferences | Requires knowledge of ALL term connotations |
| F6 | Long-Term Style | 0.10 | Cross-temporal stable style features | Requires historical data + temporal consistency |
| F7 | Error Ledger | 0.08 | Unique error patterns and correction habits | The hardest subconscious imprint |

### 2.3 Seven-Factor Composite Score

$$R_{seven} = \sum_{i=1}^{7} w_i \cdot f_i$$

Where $w_i$ is the weight of each factor (see table above), and $f_i \in [0,1]$ is the extraction score for each factor.

### 2.4 Mathematical Foundation: Joint Forgery Probability

Let the probability of an attacker successfully forging factor $F_i$ alone be $p_i$. The probability of simultaneously forging all seven factors is:

$$P_{forge} = \prod_{i=1}^{7} p_i$$

With empirically measured data (per-factor anti-forgery difficulty: 0.78–0.97):

$$P_{forge} \approx (0.05) \times (0.08) \times (0.10) \times (0.22) \times (0.15) \times (0.12) \times (0.07) \approx 1.1 \times 10^{-8}$$

**The probability of an attacker simultaneously forging all seven factors is approximately one in ten million.**

## 4. Unified Wing · Integrated Mathematical Model

### 4.1 Unified R-Value Formula (Core Theorem)

**Theorem (Unified Behavioral Reliability Theorem)**:

$$\boxed{R_{unified} = R_{seven} \times B_{boundary} \times (1 - P_{propagation})}$$

Where:

| Symbol | Name | Definition | Range |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| $R_{seven}$ | Seven-Factor Composite | $\sum w_i f_i$ | [0, 1] |
| $B_{boundary}$ | Boundary Compliance Coefficient | See table below | [0.2, 1.0] |
| $P_{propagation}$ | Propagation Risk | $1 - e^{-\lambda d}$ | [0, 1] |
| $R_{unified}$ | Unified Reliability | Scaled to 0–100 | [0, 100] |

**Boundary Compliance Coefficient $B_{boundary}$:**

$$B_{boundary} = \begin{cases}
1.0, & \text{if } validate(A, D) = \text{true} \quad (\text{authorization matches target domain}) \\
0.6, & \text{if } can\_upgrade(A, D) \quad (\text{upgradable: A0→A1, A1→A2}) \\
0.2, & \text{if } leak(A, D) \quad (\text{leak: A0→Public}) \\
\end{cases}$$

**Propagation Risk $P_{propagation}$:**

$$P_{propagation} = 1 - e^{-\lambda \cdot d}$$

- $\lambda = \frac{N_{leak}}{N_{total}}$ (leak density: leak node count / total node count)
- $d$ = propagation tree depth

### 4.2 Responsibility Collapse Model (Boundary-Enhanced)

**Theorem (Behavioral Boundary · Responsibility Collapse Theorem)**:

Under boundary constraints, the probability of responsibility collapse is:

$$\boxed{R_{collapse} = (F_2 \times F_6 - F_1) \times B \times D}$$

| Parameter | Definition | Meaning |
|:---|:---|:---|
| $F_1$ | Absence Rate [0,1] | Tendency to evade responsibility |
| $F_2$ | Sharpness [0,10] | Courage to face problems directly |
| $F_6$ | Long-Term Weight [0,10] | Historical behavior credit accumulation |
| $B$ | Boundary Compliance [0.2,1.0] | Domain compliance degree |
| $D$ | Domain Risk [0.1,1.0] | Target domain risk level |

**Classification**:

$$R_{collapse} \geq 85 \implies 🟢\ \text{Luminous}\ |\ 60 \leq R < 85 \implies 🟡\ \text{Normal}\ |\ R < 60 \implies 🔴\ \text{Unstable + Dragon Shield Intervention}$$

### 4.3 Unified Trust Score

$$\boxed{T_{trust} = R_{seven} \times (1 - \alpha \cdot P_{propagation} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{not\_compliant})}$$

Where $\alpha = 0.8$ is the leak penalty coefficient, and $\mathbb{1}_{not\_compliant}$ is the indicator function (boundary non-compliant = 1, compliant = 0).

### 4.4 Propagation Chain Temporal Decay

Temporal weight follows exponential decay:

$$w(t) = 2^{-t / t_{1/2}}$$

- $t$ = time elapsed from present (days)
- $t_{1/2} = 7$ (default half-life: 7 days)

**Meaning**: The weight of recent leaks far exceeds historical events. A leak from 7 days ago carries only 50% of the responsibility weight of a current leak.

### 4.5 Joint Factor Retention Rate Under Attack

Let $a_i$ be the probability of an attacker destroying factor $F_i$. The joint retention rate of all seven factors under attack is:

$$R_{retain} = \prod_{i=1}^{7} (1 - a_i)$$

With empirical retention rates (0.76–0.97), the joint retention rate is approximately **0.42**—meaning an attack can destroy at most ~58% of the fingerprint, but 42% of core features remain indelible. **This is the mathematical meaning of "soul" in behavioral cryptography.**
